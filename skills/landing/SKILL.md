# Landing

Select this skill from the task's need for feasibility judgment, without requiring its name. Do not reopen a settled scope during ordinary implementation or treat feasibility advice as permission to change the user's goal.

Turn a recoverable proposed direction into a feasible target without discarding its useful ambition. If the direction cannot be recovered from context, ask for it instead of guessing.

Evaluate important elements against concrete value: preserving the core upside, protecting a real external contract, reducing the largest risk, enabling cheap verification, or clarifying a stage boundary and stop rule. Keep or rewrite elements that meet those criteria; defer or remove elements that do not.

Return a grounded target shape with its users or consumers, boundaries, tradeoffs, migration constraints, largest risks, cheap success and failure signals, and the evidence that should pause, shrink, or reject it. Distinguish real constraints from caution or inertia. Do not turn this into a file-by-file implementation plan or require a fixed heading set.

For a complex migration or high-risk direction, read [references/anti-patterns.md](references/anti-patterns.md) and apply only the relevant counter-moves.

A joint takeoff-and-landing request is valid in one turn: apply takeoff first, then land the preferred thesis. User disagreement becomes a new constraint to price, not a reason to stop or to accept it without examining cost and risk.
